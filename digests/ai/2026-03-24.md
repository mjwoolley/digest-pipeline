[**Trillion-Parameter Models Are Now Running on a MacBook Pro**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/streaming-experts/#atom-everything)
The "streaming experts" technique — loading only the MoE expert weights needed per token from SSD rather than holding the full model in RAM — has hit a wild new milestone: Kimi K2.5, a 1T-parameter model with 32B active weights, is running on an M2 Max MacBook Pro with 96GB RAM. Meanwhile, the 397B Qwen3.5 model is running on an iPhone at 0.6 tokens/second, with a public iOS repo available.
_Why it matters: Consumer hardware running trillion-parameter models shatters assumptions about who can run frontier-scale AI locally — and the optimization work is still accelerating._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/streaming-experts/#atom-everything)
