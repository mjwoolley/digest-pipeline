[**Anthropic Lets Claude Take the Wheel of Your Mac**](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2036195789601374705)
Anthropic's research preview gives Claude direct desktop control — clicks, typing, and app navigation — with the Dispatch feature letting users assign tasks remotely from their phone. The system prioritizes direct app integrations over screen control, only resorting to clicking when necessary. Currently limited to macOS Pro/Max users via Cowork and Claude Code, with Windows support coming.
_Why it matters: This is the first product out of Anthropic's Vercept acquisition, and it marks a serious step toward autonomous AI agents operating on real computers without constant human supervision._
→ [The Rundown AI](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2036195789601374705)

[**ByteDance's DeerFlow 2.0 Is a Full-Stack Agentic Research Engine**](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow)
ByteDance open-sourced DeerFlow 2.0, a SuperAgent framework capable of handling multi-hour tasks spanning research, coding, and content creation. It leverages sandboxes, memory, tools, skills, subagents, and a message gateway to coordinate complex workflows. Supports Python 3.12+ and Node.js 22+.
_Why it matters: A production-grade, open-source agentic harness from ByteDance puts serious multi-agent infrastructure in the hands of any developer, no enterprise contract required._
→ [GitHub Trending](https://github.com/bytedance/deer-flow)
