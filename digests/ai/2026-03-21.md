_Why it matters: These are practical, unsolved problems at the frontier of agentic coding — Karpathy naming them publicly will accelerate focus from the community._

_Why it matters: Language shapes professional norms — getting the terminology right early helps distinguish disciplined agent-assisted development from throwaway prototyping._
