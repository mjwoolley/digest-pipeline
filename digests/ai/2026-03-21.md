**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**Anthropic Ran the Largest-Ever Qualitative AI Study — 81K Interviews, 70 Languages**](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews)
Anthropic used Claude itself to conduct open-ended interviews with 81,000 people across 159 countries, making it the largest qualitative AI attitudes study on record. Top hopes centered on professional excellence and reclaiming time; top fear was AI getting things wrong, ahead of job loss or loss of agency. Sentiment skewed positive in India and South America, neutral-to-negative in the U.S., Europe, Japan, and South Korea.
_Why it matters: Using Claude to run 80K in-depth interviews in a single week is a compelling proof point for agentic research at scale — and the regional sentiment splits have real policy implications._
→ [The Rundown AI](https://www.anthropic.com/features/81k-interviews)

[**Karpathy: Agents Write Ugly Code and AI Needs a Soul, Not Just Safety Rails**](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2035173492447224237)
