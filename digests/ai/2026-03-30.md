[**Anthropic Study: The More AI Helps You, the More You Fear It**](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2034302163720294690)
Anthropic research finds that benefit and fear from AI are tightly correlated — the people gaining the most from AI in a domain are also the most anxious about its risks there. Benefits were grounded in lived experience while fears were largely anticipatory; economic anxiety was the single strongest predictor of negative AI sentiment overall. On the bright side, 81% of respondents felt AI had already moved toward fulfilling their personal vision for it.
_Why it matters: Understanding that AI optimism and pessimism co-exist in the same people — rather than splitting the population — should reshape how labs and policymakers communicate about AI risk._
→ [@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2034302163720294690)
