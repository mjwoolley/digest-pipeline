**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**Karpathy's autoresearch Lets AI Run Its Own LLM Training Experiments**](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch)
Andrej Karpathy released autoresearch, enabling AI agents to autonomously run and iterate on LLM training experiments in a loop on a single GPU. Over ~two days at depth=12, the agent ran ~650 experiments, found ~20 meaningful improvements, and cut the 'Time to GPT-2' benchmark from 2.02 to 1.80 hours — an 11% gain — with all changes verified to transfer to larger models.
_Why it matters: Automated ML research is moving from concept to concrete results; an agent independently discovering optimizer misalignment and initialization improvements is a preview of AI-accelerated science._
→ [@karpathy](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch)

[**Context Rot: The Silent Performance Killer in Enterprise AI**](https://thenewstack.io/context-rot-enterprise-ai-llms/)
As enterprises feed ever-larger data pools to AI agents and LLMs, new data conflicting with older data causes "context rot" — diluting output quality and introducing contradictions that are hard to detect. It's emerging as one of the most insidious failure modes in production AI deployments.
_Why it matters: Unlike hallucination, context rot degrades gradually and silently, making it easy to miss until business-critical outputs are already compromised._
→ [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/context-rot-enterprise-ai-llms/)

[**New LLMs Handle Niche Tools Better Than We Thought — Context Windows Are the Key**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/9/not-so-boring/#atom-everything)
Earlier fears that coding agents would bias technology choices toward whatever dominated their training data appear overstated. Latest models with long context windows can ingest substantial documentation at inference time and work effectively with new, private, or niche libraries.
_Why it matters: If models can learn novel tools on the fly from docs, the "LLMs enforce boring tech stacks" concern largely dissolves — opening the door to AI-assisted adoption of cutting-edge tooling._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/9/not-so-boring/#atom-everything)

[**PostgreSQL 18 Lets You Clone Production Query Plans Without the Data**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/9/production-query-plans-without-production-data/#atom-everything)
PostgreSQL 18 introduces pg_restore_relation_stats() and pg_restore_attribute_stats(), allowing developers to copy database statistics from production to dev environments without moving actual data. Statistics dumps are typically under 1MB even for databases with hundreds of tables, making production-accurate query plan debugging trivially easy.
_Why it matters: Debugging slow queries without production data has been a persistent pain point; this elegant solution removes a major barrier to catching performance regressions before they ship._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/9/production-query-plans-without-production-data/#atom-everything)
