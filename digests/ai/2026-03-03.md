**🚀 Model Releases**

• **Inception Labs' Mercury 2 hits 1,000+ tokens/sec with diffusion architecture**
Inception Labs launched Mercury 2, an LLM built on diffusion architecture rather than traditional autoregressive processing. Instead of generating tokens sequentially, it iterates in parallel — the same principle behind image diffusion models — achieving over 1,000 tokens per second, far outpacing Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
_Why it matters: If diffusion-based LLMs can match autoregressive models on quality while running 10x faster, the cost and latency assumptions underlying most AI applications get rewritten._
→ [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/inception-labs-mercury-2-diffusion/)
