**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**LLMs Are Bloat Machines — And That's a Human Problem**](https://bcantrill.dtrace.org/2026/04/12/the-peril-of-laziness-lost/)
Bryan Cantrill argues that LLMs are structurally incapable of the productive laziness that drives good software engineering — the drive to build crisp abstractions because sloppy ones cost *you* time later. Since generating code is effortless for a model, LLMs default to more: more layers, more complexity, more mess.
_Why it matters: This reframes a core risk of AI-assisted development — not hallucination, but unchecked bloat — and puts the burden of taste and restraint squarely back on human engineers._
→ [Simon Willison](https://bcantrill.dtrace.org/2026/04/12/the-peril-of-laziness-lost/)
