**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**Netflix's VOID Doesn't Just Erase Objects — It Rewrites Their Physics**](https://void-model.github.io/)
Netflix Research has open-sourced VOID, a framework that goes far beyond inpainting by reasoning about the physical consequences of removing objects from video — think a balloon floating away when its holder disappears, or a block staying put when one upstream in a chain is erased. It uses a structured mask to distinguish what to remove, what's physically affected, and what to preserve, then runs a judge model to propagate consequences through the scene. In head-to-head evaluations against six baselines including Runway, human evaluators preferred VOID's output nearly two-thirds of the time.
_Why it matters: This is Netflix Research's first public AI release and a signal that production-grade video editing is moving toward physics-aware simulation, not just pixel-level patching._
→ [The Rundown AI](https://void-model.github.io/)
