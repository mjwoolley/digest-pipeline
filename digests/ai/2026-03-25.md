[**Package Managers Finally Adding Dependency Cooldowns**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/package-managers-need-to-cool-down/#atom-everything)
Prompted by today's LiteLLM attack, Simon Willison revisits dependency cooldowns — the practice of waiting a few days before installing newly published packages to let the community catch poisoned versions. A flurry of major tooling updates since late 2025 has added this capability: pnpm, Yarn, Bun, Deno, uv, and pip 26.0 all now ship some form of minimum release age enforcement. The features vary in configurability but the direction is clear: the ecosystem is taking supply chain hygiene seriously at the tooling level.
_Why it matters: Cooldown defaults baked into package managers are the scalable defense against the class of attack that burned LiteLLM users today._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/package-managers-need-to-cool-down/#atom-everything)

**💼 Industry & Business**

[**LiteLLM PyPI Supply Chain Attack Exposes Millions of AI Projects**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/malicious-litellm/#atom-everything)
LiteLLM versions 1.82.7 and 1.82.8 on PyPI were compromised with a credential stealer hidden in base64 inside a `.pth` file — meaning merely *installing* the package, without ever importing it, was enough to trigger exfiltration of SSH keys, AWS/GCP/Azure credentials, Kubernetes configs, shell history, crypto wallets, and more. The poisoned version was live for roughly 3 hours before PyPI quarantined it, with estimated exposure in the 20K–80K download range; the blast radius extended to every project transitively depending on litellm, including DSPy. The attack was discovered only because it caused an out-of-memory crash on one developer's machine — a lucky bug in the malware itself.
_Why it matters: With 97M downloads/month, litellm is load-bearing infrastructure for the Python AI ecosystem — this attack demonstrates that transitive dependencies are the soft underbelly of every AI project's security posture._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/24/malicious-litellm/#atom-everything), [@karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2036487306585268612), [Latent Space](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47501426)
