[**Karpathy Names the "AI Psychosis" Divide Splitting Users Into Two Camps**](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415)
Andrej Karpathy identified a sharp and growing perception gap in AI capability: casual users on free/old models see incremental, unimpressive improvements in search and writing, while professionals paying for frontier agentic models in coding, math, and research are watching those models dissolve week-long problems in hours. The divide comes down to reinforcement learning's uneven progress — domains with verifiable reward functions (unit tests, proofs) have seen explosive gains; open-ended tasks haven't. The two groups, Karpathy notes, literally can't have a coherent conversation about AI progress.
_Why it matters: This framing is the clearest explanation yet for why AI discourse feels so fractured — and why benchmarks on writing and search hide the most dramatic capability leaps happening right now._
→ [@karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2042334451611693415)
