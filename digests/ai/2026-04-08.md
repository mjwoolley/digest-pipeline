**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**Agent Memory: The Missing Layer Between Chatbot and Colleague**](https://theaiengineer.substack.com/p/what-is-agent-memory)
A Salesforce 2025 benchmark found AI agents resolved only 35% of multi-turn customer support tasks end-to-end, with context loss being the top failure mode — agents forget names, issues, and prior workarounds the moment a session ends. The article breaks down agent memory into three layers: short-term (within-session), long-term (cross-session persistence), and the context window (what the model actually reads). It argues that meaningful agent deployment requires treating memory as a first-class architectural concern, not an afterthought.
_Why it matters: Until persistent memory is solved, even the best frontier models are structurally limited to stateless interactions — a ceiling that blocks most high-value enterprise use cases._
→ [The AI Engineer](https://theaiengineer.substack.com/p/what-is-agent-memory)
