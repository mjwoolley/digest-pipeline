**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**What is Agentic Engineering? Simon Willison Publishes Foundational Guide**](https://simonwillison.net/guides/agentic-engineering-patterns/what-is-agentic-engineering/#atom-everything)
Simon Willison published the opening chapter of his Agentic Engineering Patterns guide, defining agentic engineering as building software with the assistance of coding agents — tools that can both write and execute code. He frames the agent loop clearly: prompt an LLM with tool definitions, execute the tools it requests, feed results back, and repeat until working software emerges.
_Why it matters: Clean, practitioner-level definitions matter as "agentic" becomes an overloaded buzzword — Willison's framing gives the field a grounded vocabulary._
