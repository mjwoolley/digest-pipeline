**🌐 Other AI News**

• **XZ Utils Backdoor Exposed a Critical Open-Source Security Gap**
A backdoor planted inside XZ Utils — a compression library embedded in major Linux distributions — nearly gave attackers full SSH admin access to millions of systems worldwide. The attacker, Jia Tan, spent two years cultivating trust as a maintainer before inserting the malicious code, and was caught only by accident when a Microsoft engineer noticed anomalous SSH latency. The incident has since sparked renewed efforts around open-source governance frameworks to prevent long-term infiltration of critical dependencies.
_Why it matters: A single patient attacker almost compromised foundational Linux infrastructure globally — it's a wake-up call that open-source trust models are dangerously underresourced._
→ [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/commonhaus-open-source-governance/)
