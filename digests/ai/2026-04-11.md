Oxford researchers built an AI that detects subtle texture changes in heart fat from routine CT scans, identifying patients at high risk of heart failure up to five years early with 86% accuracy across 72,000 patients. In the highest-risk group, 1 in 4 developed heart failure within five years — a 20x gap compared to patients the AI flagged as safe. Oxford is in regulatory talks to deploy the tool across NHS hospitals and plans to extend it to all chest CTs within months.
_Why it matters: Turning a scan people already get into a five-year early warning system could fundamentally shift heart failure from a reactive crisis to a preventable condition._
