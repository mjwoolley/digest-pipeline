**🏗️ Infrastructure & Platforms**

[**Running a 209GB Model on a MacBook With 5.5GB of Active Memory**](https://x.com/danveloper/status/2034353876753592372)
Dan Veloper ran Qwen 3.5 397B-A17B — a 209GB MoE model — on an M3 Mac at 5.7 tokens/second by streaming quantized weights from SSD at ~17GB/s, keeping only 5.5GB resident in memory at a time. The trick exploits MoE architecture: since only a small subset of weights fire per token, you only need to load what's active.
_Why it matters: This approach, grounded in Apple's "LLM in a Flash" research, suggests that local inference of frontier-scale models is closer than it appears — no data center required._
→ [danveloper](https://x.com/danveloper/status/2034353876753592372)

[**The Case for Kubernetes as the Backbone of Open-Source AI Infrastructure**](https://thenewstack.io/kubernetes-open-source-ai/)
As open models like DeepSeek, Llama, and Mistral proliferate, the argument is that AI infrastructure must be open-source to keep pace. Kubernetes is positioned as the natural foundation, but gaps remain in GPU resource management, hardware description, and specialized AI workload support.
_Why it matters: Who controls AI infrastructure shapes who benefits from the open-model wave — closed infra under open models is a half-measure._
→ [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/kubernetes-open-source-ai/)

**🔬 Research & Techniques**

[**90 Experiments Later: Running a 397B Model Locally With Apple's Flash Inference Technique**](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/18/llm-in-a-flash/#atom-everything)
Dan Woods ran Qwen3.5-397B-A17B at 5.5+ tokens/second on a 48GB MacBook Pro M3 Max by implementing Apple's 2023 "LLM in a Flash" paper, which pages model weights from flash storage to DRAM on demand. He used Claude Code to run 90 automated experiments generating MLX Objective-C and Metal code, with the resulting paper itself largely written by Claude Opus 4.6.
_Why it matters: The experiment is a proof point both for flash-based inference viability and for using AI agents to accelerate low-level systems research — the methodology may be as significant as the result._
→ [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Mar/18/llm-in-a-flash/#atom-everything)
